A forkable 11ty site that turns [dev.to](https://dev.to/winstonpuckett) into the CMS for your blog.

## Impetus

One day I decided I wanted to write a blog. I didn't want to maintain my own CMS,
so I did some research and landed on [dev.to](https://dev.to/winstonpuckett) to
host my Markdown posts. After finding there were no good blog generators for
dev.to at the time, I decided to write my own.

## Features

- **Incredibly performant** — an SSG ships only raw HTML/CSS/JS, so every page load scores well.
- **A search bar** for finding specific posts and topics.
- **Full control** — just fork the repo.
- **Easy integrations** with [Google Analytics](https://analytics.google.com), [AdSense](https://www.google.com/adsense/start/), and [Coil](https://coil.com/).
- **Social links** for [dev.to](https://dev.to), [GitHub](https://github.com), and Twitter.
